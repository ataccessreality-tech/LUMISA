Restaurant

Our restaurant offers a curated dining experience featuring local specialties and international cuisine. Start your day with our signature breakfast buffet, enjoy business lunches with fresh ingredients, or unwind with evening dining in a sophisticated atmosphere. Our chefs combine regional flavors with contemporary presentation, ensuring every meal complements your stay.

From seasonal menus featuring local ingredients to 24-hour room service, we ensure every dining need is met with quality and care. Our attentive staff and flexible dining hours make every meal convenient, whether you're grabbing a quick bite or settling in for a relaxed dinner.

Health & Beauty

Rejuvenate body and mind in our calm, expertly staffed wellness spaces. Enjoy therapeutic massages, revitalizing facials, and refreshing beauty treatments delivered with premium products and personalized attention. Open early to late, our services fit your schedule—perfect for a quick refresh between meetings or a full afternoon of pampering.

Swimming Pool

Take a refreshing dip in our amazing pool, accessible all day, Surrounded by comfortable loungers and shaded cabanas, it’s ideal for morning laps, an afternoon cool-down, or a quiet evening unwind. Towels, lockers, and gentle background music complete the relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere.

Conference Room

Host seamless meetings in our fully equipped conference space, featuring high-speed Wi-Fi and adaptable layouts for guests. On-site tech support, whiteboard walls, and complimentary stationery keep sessions running smoothly. Half-day and full-day packages include freshly brewed coffee, chilled water, and catered meal options—everything you need to impress clients or collaborate with colleagues under one roof.
